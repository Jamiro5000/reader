How it works: pressing and holding the Action Button does exactly what tapping Start / Stop does. Start turns red and says ■ Stop, and the top-left badge shows ● REC. About two seconds later a banner at the top of the screen confirms it.

Ready is optional. It switches the camera on early but does NOT record (the badge says “Camera on · not recording”). Tap it before going full screen so the first press starts instantly.

Works on any Wi-Fi or on mobile data. Your Mac isn't needed.

One-time setup: open your setup link on this phone once, add the Record shortcut, then in iPhone Settings → Action Button choose Shortcut → Record.
