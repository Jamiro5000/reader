Action Button

Checking…

Press once: the camera and microphone switch on and recording starts (the top-left badge shows ● REC). Press again: recording stops and the camera and microphone switch off. A banner confirms each press about two seconds later.

Keep this page on screen. iPhone only lets a page use the camera while you can see the page. A video in iPhone's own full screen covers the page, so iPhone pauses the camera and microphone then: a press can't start a recording, and if you're already recording, that part of the clip freezes and goes quiet (the clip is marked ⚠ under 🎞). Leave full screen before you press.

Saving: clips wait under 🎞 in the bar. When you're done, tap 🎞 → Save all to Photos.

Tip: in Safari tap aA → Website Settings and set Camera and Microphone to Allow, so iPhone never stops to ask.

One-time setup: open your setup link on this phone once, add the Record shortcut, then in iPhone Settings → Action Button choose Shortcut → Record.

Your clips

This tab can’t keep clips once the page closes (it may be a Private tab). Save them before closing it, and use a normal Safari tab next time.

Clips are kept on this phone (inside Safari, for this page) until you save them, even if the page reloads. Clips already saved to Photos are removed from here after 3 days.
